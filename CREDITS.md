# Asset credits

The Office page (`/office`) renders pixel art adapted from open projects:

- **Characters** (`public/office/characters/char_0..5.png`): from
  [pixel-agents](https://github.com/pablodelucca/pixel-agents) (MIT, Pablo De
  Lucca), themselves based on the
  [MetroCity free top-down character pack](https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack)
  by **JIK-A-4**. Thank you!
- **Furniture, floors and walls** (packed into `public/office/atlas.png` by
  `scripts/pack-office-atlas.py`): office assets bundled open-source in
  [pixel-agents](https://github.com/pablodelucca/pixel-agents) (MIT).
- **Behavior patterns** (state machine, wander loop, BFS pathing, z-sorting)
  are ports inspired by pixel-agents (MIT) and
  [agents-in-the-office](https://github.com/gukosowa/agents-in-the-office) (MIT).

Everything else in this repository is original to CLARMY (MIT).
