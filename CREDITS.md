# Asset credits

The Office page (`/office`) renders pixel art adapted from open projects:

- **Office backdrop** (`public/office/office-bg-{dark,light}.png`): original
  CLARMY pixel-art floor plan, generated by `scripts/gen-office-background.py`
  (MIT, this repo).
- **Characters** (`public/office/characters/agent_*.png`): original CLARMY
  provider personas (Grok, Claude, Gemini, Copilot/Codex), generated by
  `scripts/gen-agent-sprites.py` (MIT, this repo). Legacy clawd sheets
  (`clawd_0..5.png`) remain for reference.
- **Themed decor** (skull candles, knight banners, Chinese-AI posters, etc.):
  appended to `public/office/atlas.png` by `scripts/gen-office-theme-decor.py`
  (MIT, this repo).
- **Furniture, floors and walls** (packed into `public/office/atlas.png` by
  `scripts/pack-office-atlas.py`): office assets bundled open-source in
  [pixel-agents](https://github.com/pablodelucca/pixel-agents) (MIT).
- **Behavior patterns** (state machine, wander loop, BFS pathing, z-sorting)
  are ports inspired by pixel-agents (MIT) and
  [agents-in-the-office](https://github.com/gukosowa/agents-in-the-office) (MIT).

Everything else in this repository is original to CLARMY (MIT).
